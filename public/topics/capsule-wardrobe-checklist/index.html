The 30-piece capsule wardrobe checklist for beginners

A simple checklist to build a wardrobe you actually wear, without buying everything at once.

  • Start with what you already own
  • Pick one neutral and one accent colour
  • Aim for 5 tops, 3 bottoms, 2 layers
  • Count outfits, not items
  • Add one piece only when you find a gap
