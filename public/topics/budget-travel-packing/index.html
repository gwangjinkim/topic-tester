Carry-on only packing list for a weekend trip

Pack light without forgetting the things that matter. Works for trains, planes and buses.

  • Choose a bag before you choose clothes
  • Roll, don't fold, for soft items
  • Wear the bulky layer on travel day
  • Limit toiletries to a single pouch
  • Leave 20% of the bag empty
